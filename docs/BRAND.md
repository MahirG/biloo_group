# Biloo Brand System

## Positioning

Biloo is a premium, minimal, technical, and human technology brand rooted in Ethiopia and designed for global relevance.

## Core palette

| Color               | Hex       | Primary use                                |
| ------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Midnight Graphite   | `#0F172A` | Primary backgrounds, typography, authority |
| Royal Sapphire Blue | `#1E3A8A` | Signature accent, interaction, emphasis    |
| Ivory White         | `#F8FAFC` | Space, clarity, contrast                   |

Tints and opacity variations may be derived from these colors for interface states. New permanent brand colors should not be introduced casually.

## Signature mark

The double “oo” is Biloo's primary visual idea. It may appear as:

- Two connected circles
- A restrained infinity reference
- Two modules exchanging value
- A continuity or generational symbol

The mark must remain geometric, simple, legible at small sizes, and free of generic technology clichés.

## Typography

The initial digital typeface is **Geist** because it is clear, contemporary, and highly readable. A custom wordmark may be developed later.

Headlines should use tight tracking, strong hierarchy, and short statements. Body copy should remain calm, direct, and accessible.

## Voice

Biloo speaks with confidence without exaggeration.

Use:

- “We are building…”
- “Strategic direction”
- “In development”
- “Designed to…”

Avoid unsupported language such as:

- “Africa's leading”
- “Revolutionary”
- “World's best”
- “Trusted by millions”

## Tagline

Primary: **Technology built for generations.**

Supporting lines:

- Ethiopia-rooted. Future-built.
- Build something worthy of the name it carries.
- African context. Global standards.

## Experience principles

1. Make the important action obvious.
2. Use motion to clarify, not decorate.
3. Keep interfaces fast on modest devices and connections.
4. Meet WCAG AA accessibility as a baseline.
5. Avoid copying the visual identity of major technology companies.
