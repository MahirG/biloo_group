# Biloo Group Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation

**Objective:** Establish a credible company identity and operating system.

- Confirm company spelling and domain ownership
- Complete trademark and company-name clearance
- Establish legal entity and verified contact details
- Publish the initial website and foundation documents
- Create brand assets and an identity usage guide
- Define decision records, security policy, and repository standards

## Phase 1 — Problem selection and validation

**Objective:** Select one valuable problem rather than launching many product labels.

- Interview target users and institutions
- Score opportunities by urgency, market access, regulatory difficulty, founder advantage, and revenue potential
- Test demand with prototypes and manual services
- Choose one flagship product thesis
- Define success metrics and explicit stop conditions

## Phase 2 — Flagship product

**Objective:** Build and operate one dependable product.

- Create product requirements and system architecture
- Ship a narrow, useful minimum product
- Measure activation, retention, reliability, and willingness to pay
- Establish support, privacy, security, and incident processes
- Document what is repeatable before expanding

## Phase 3 — Platform capability

**Objective:** Turn proven capabilities into reusable company infrastructure.

- Shared identity and access management
- Payments and billing where legally appropriate
- Observability and incident management
- Design system and component library
- Data governance and analytics standards

## Phase 4 — Group expansion

**Objective:** Launch or acquire additional businesses only when justified.

A new Biloo product line should require:

1. A validated customer problem
2. A responsible executive owner
3. Dedicated funding and operating capacity
4. Legal and regulatory clearance
5. A product strategy that benefits from the Biloo platform
6. Evidence that expansion will not weaken the flagship business

## First 90-day priorities

1. Finalize name, domain, and legal checks.
2. Publish website version 1.
3. Conduct at least 20 structured problem interviews.
4. Select the first flagship opportunity.
5. Produce a written product thesis and validation plan.
