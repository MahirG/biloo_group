# Biloo Group Foundation

## Company identity

**Name:** Biloo Group  
**Public brand:** Biloo  
**Founder & CEO:** Mahir Aman  
**Origin:** Ethiopia  
**Stage:** Foundation and validation

Biloo Group is being established as a long-term African technology company. Its purpose is to build dependable digital platforms that improve how people, businesses, and institutions work.

## Origin of the name

Biloo is inspired by **Bilo**, the name of founder Mahir Aman's great-grandfather. It is an Afaan Oromo-rooted family name carried into a modern company identity.

The adapted spelling, **Biloo**, preserves the family connection while creating a distinct brand. The paired “oo” becomes the visual signature of the company, representing continuity, partnership, connection, and a horizon beyond one founder or one product.

This story must be communicated with dignity. It is not a marketing invention; it is the company’s legacy foundation.

## Vision

To become one of Africa's most respected technology groups by solving real problems with long-term discipline, African context, and global engineering standards.

## Mission

Build dependable digital platforms that improve how people, businesses, and institutions work.

## Operating thesis

Biloo Group will not begin by pretending to operate many mature companies. It will begin by identifying a meaningful problem, validating demand, building one focused product, earning trust, and expanding only when evidence and organizational capability justify expansion.

The group structure is a long-term architecture, not a reason to fragment attention today.

## Active project portfolio

### Qabeza ERP

Qabeza ERP is Biloo Group's enterprise resource planning project for organizations that need a unified view of finance, procurement, inventory, payroll, operations, and governance. Its intended scope includes multi-branch operations, roles and approvals, audit logs, procurement and payroll workflows, and configurable integrations.

**Status:** Product project under validation and development. Public material must not imply production readiness, customers, or integrations until verified.

### Mezgeb

Mezgeb is an Ethiopian business-ledger project for small businesses. Its product direction covers sales, expenses, VAT-ready receipts, customer credit or Dube, payment tracking, inventory, suppliers, reports, and daily business control.

**Status:** Interactive prototype and early product development. It must not be described as suitable for real financial records until security, data protection, reliability, and operational readiness are verified. The planned project domain is `mezgeb.com.et`.

## Strategic fields

Biloo may explore the following fields over time:

- Artificial intelligence and multilingual computing
- Cloud and developer infrastructure
- Financial technology and payments
- Digital commerce, marketplaces, and logistics enablement
- Business operating systems and enterprise software
- Public-sector digital services
- Product design and applied research

Product-line names including Biloo AI, Biloo Cloud, Biloo Pay, Biloo Commerce, Biloo Labs, and Biloo Gov are working strategic directions. Each requires separate market validation, legal review, financial planning, regulatory analysis, and a credible operating team before launch.

## Founder profile

Mahir Aman is the Founder and CEO of Biloo Group. His stated areas of interest include technology, artificial intelligence, cloud systems, digital commerce, fintech, marketplaces, public-sector digital systems, dropshipping and cross-border commerce, and financial markets.

Public biographies must remain accurate. They may describe demonstrated work, active study, and stated interests, but must not invent credentials, awards, partnerships, customers, revenue, or institutional endorsements.

## Brand promise

Biloo will aim to be:

- Dependable before impressive
- Clear before complex
- Useful before fashionable
- Secure before convenient
- Accessible before exclusive
- Ambitious without exaggeration

## Contact and address policy

The company may publicly state **Ethiopia** as its origin. A street address and telephone number must not be published until the founder provides verified business contact details.

The founder email is `mahir@biloogroup.com`. It should be published only after domain ownership and email delivery are verified.

## Governance principles

1. Separate company facts from future ambition.
2. Document major product, architecture, security, and brand decisions.
3. Use written criteria before entering regulated sectors.
4. Protect customer data by default.
5. Avoid naming a product before validating the problem it solves.
6. Preserve institutional knowledge so the company can outlast its founder.
